Video 005 — V-005 analysis and sensitivity¶

tl;dr¶

The frozen 2001→2023 primary comparison has 161 pair-comparable countries and areas. Among 133 with a robust obesity rise, PoU fell robustly in 81, rose robustly in 28 and was directionally indeterminate in 24.

The frozen rule p_F > p_R > 0 holds in the primary result and in all 13 registered sensitivity scenarios. An implementation that does not import the primary analysis reproduced every pair classification and scenario: 28/28 validation checks pass.

This notebook is an internal scientific artifact. It does not authorize public copy, storyboard, render, site changes, upload or publication.

Question, hypotheses and scope¶

Question: from 2001 to 2023, as adult obesity rose robustly, did undernourishment more often rise with it or fall?

  • H1 requires p_F > p_R > 0 in the primary endpoint comparison.
  • H2 requires the same ordering in every V-004 sensitivity plus independent reproduction.

This is descriptive ecological co-movement, not causal attribution and not a same-people comparison. The hypotheses were data-informed before the source, quality, method and sensitivity contracts were frozen.

Decision and limitations¶

  • H1: SUPPORTED by the frozen primary rule.
  • H2: SUPPORTED after all 13 frozen sensitivities and independent reproduction.
  • The result is conditional on comparable countries and areas.
  • National co-movement is not causality and does not describe the same people.
  • Obesity and PoU cover different populations and uncertainty structures.
  • Adjacent PoU midpoint frames overlap by two years.
  • PoU censoring and small regional denominators must remain visible.
  • Population-weighted shares are descriptive only and do not enter H1/H2.
  • Twelve endpoint entities are off-map but remain in analytical counts.
