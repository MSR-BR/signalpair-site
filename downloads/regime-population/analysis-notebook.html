Conclusions and limits¶

  • H1 is supported under the frozen Q0 endpoint rule.
  • H2 is supported under all four frozen classifications and the conservative missing-population bounds.
  • The co-housed 2024 scientific endpoint gate passes and independent validation reports 23/23 checks.
  • The early adjacent-year diagnostic fails: the 1973 positive sign does not persist through 1975. The late endpoint is stable from 2023 through 2025.
  • Therefore a future public artifact may be eligible to show a non-linear reversal, but must not present it as gradual, monotonic, causal or uniformly global.
  • S10 external classification and S11 source revision remain not testable because no compatible external classification or second official snapshot was contracted.

These are ecological, descriptive comparisons between denominators. They do not show that regime type causes population change, do not describe individual political experience, and do not rank countries morally.
